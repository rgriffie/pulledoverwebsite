03 / THE INFORMATION GAP

Information

  1. Information influences decisions, actions, and outcomes.

    Organizations increasingly rely on intelligent systems to produce information, inform decisions, and drive outcomes before the organization can determine whether that information is ready to be relied upon.

  2. Information moves faster than anyone can verify it.

    Intelligent systems now generate summaries, recommendations, and forecasts continuously. That information flows straight into the work people do, often without anyone pausing to ask where it came from.

  3. Information becomes consequential BEFORE information readiness becomes known.
